# Podcast artwork

podcast-hero.png was generated with the built-in image generation tool on October 23, 2026. The user supplied the podcast cover as a visual reference and authorized a new interpretation.

Prompt: Create a transparent-background flat editorial illustration for the From Paychecks to Purpose podcast website. Inspired by the supplied cover: woman with long black hair, turquoise blue skin, round golden glasses, golden yellow sweater and cobalt checked trousers, hands comfortably clasped. Behind her a golden sun arc and angular black mountain peaks. Black outlines, clean bold shapes, turquoise/cobalt/gold palette. Square composition with generous margins, no text, no typography, no watermark. New interpretation of the reference cover artwork, suitable for right side of a homepage hero.

The tool icons are hand-authored SVGs. The original cover was a reference, not modified or published.
