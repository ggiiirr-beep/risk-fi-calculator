Privacy & storage

Effective November 3, 2026

Purpose Fi calculates on your device. Your financial entries are not sent to us. There are no accounts, analytics, advertisements, or cross-device or website syncing in this app.

Your profiles

The calculator starts with Profile 1 and saves your numbers and calculator settings automatically on this device. You can rename it or create additional profiles. Existing remembered numbers and saved copies carry over as profiles.

In Manage profiles, Reset this profile restores its numbers and settings to defaults while keeping its name. Delete this profile removes only that profile. Delete all profiles and data removes every saved profile, current entries, and display preferences from this app. After deleting the last profile or all data, the calculator starts a fresh Profile 1. All three actions ask for confirmation. There are no accounts or syncing; other devices and the website are unaffected.

Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to see your profiles. Removing the app removes its local data. Device backups may include app data according to your system settings.

Offline use and external links

The calculator and its help are bundled with the app and work offline. If you choose an external link or contact us, the destination service processes that interaction under its own privacy policy. We cannot remotely retrieve your saved plan.
