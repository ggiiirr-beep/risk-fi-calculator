Privacy & storage

Effective November 3, 2026

Purpose Fi calculates on your device. Your financial entries are not sent to us. There are no accounts, analytics, advertisements, or cross-device or website syncing in this app.

Your saved numbers

Choose Remember Numbers to save your plan and display preferences in this app’s local storage. Without saving, entries are temporary and may be lost when the app reloads. Turning saving off or selecting Clear current numbers removes the saved plan; numbers already on screen remain until the app reloads. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to see your plan. Removing the app removes its local data. Device backups may include app data according to your system settings.

Saved sets of numbers

Save new copy stores a named snapshot of your numbers and calculator settings on this device, even if Remember Numbers is off. Saved copies change only when you update, rename, or delete them. Clearing current numbers or turning Remember Numbers off does not delete saved copies. Use Delete selected to remove a saved copy. Loading a copy replaces your current entries and turns Remember Numbers on.

Offline use and external links

The calculator and its help are bundled with the app and work offline. If you choose an external link or contact us, the destination service processes that interaction under its own privacy policy. We cannot remotely retrieve your saved plan.
