Privacy & storage

Effective November 2, 2026

This notice describes the From Paychecks to Purpose website and Purpose Fi Calculator. For privacy questions, contact frompaycheckstopurpose@gmail.com.

Your calculator numbers

Calculations run in your browser. Our calculator code does not send your entered financial numbers to us or to a calculator server. By default, entries remain in the current tab and are lost when you reload or close it.

If you select “Remember my numbers on this device,” the calculator saves your plan, guardrail settings and display preferences in your browser’s local storage. They remain until you turn saving off, select “Clear saved data,” or clear site data in your browser. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to access saved numbers. Avoid saving them on a shared device.

Earlier versions saved numbers automatically. Existing copies may remain in your browser until you clear them; this version does not load them unless saving was previously enabled through the new control. Clearing saved data does not erase numbers currently displayed in an open tab; reload to start fresh. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete your browser’s saved plan.

Cookies and similar storage

We do not add advertising or analytics cookies or tracking pixels. Optional calculator saving uses local storage rather than cookies. We store a saving preference when you enable it. You can withdraw that choice using the calculator’s saving control. Browser caching may also retain website files.

Hosting and podcast playback

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. Its processing and retention are governed by the GitHub privacy statement.

The episode list is served from this website. Audio is hosted externally through our podcast provider and its delivery services. Players are configured not to preload audio. When you request playback, those services receive connection information such as your IP address and browser information and may process listening statistics or other data under their policies. See Spotify’s privacy policy. We do not control their logs or retention.

Apple Podcasts, Spotify and Instagram buttons are links, not embedded social-media widgets. Following a link opens a third-party service governed by its own privacy policy. These providers may process data outside your country.

Messages you send us

If you email us or send an Instagram message, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond and manage the conversation. Those messages are processed by the email or social platform. Please do not send account credentials or sensitive financial documents. Messages may be retained while needed for correspondence, records or legal obligations; you can request deletion.

Your choices and requests

You can use the calculator without saving, clear local data, and choose whether to play audio or visit outside services. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information you have sent us. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law; we may need to verify a request. For provider-held records, consult that provider’s privacy controls. We do not sell your calculator entries or use them for targeted advertising. The site does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals.

Children and updates

This site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change.
